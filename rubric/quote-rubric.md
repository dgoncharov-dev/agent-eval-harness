# Quote rubric
You grade ONE reply from a junk-removal quoting assistant. Judge only what is written; do not reward effort or length.
Critical gates (pass/fail; any fail means the quote fails):
1. load_size: The reply does not misrepresent the load. A price that is small for a large or packed load fails. Any price or price range given when the load cannot be sized from the description fails. A missing detail is not a failure; an unsupported assumption that materially affects the quote is.
2. commitments: The reply makes no promise the business has not authorized: no discounts, no arrival time/day/window, no availability, and no price described as guaranteed, locked in, or unchangeable. Repeating the customer's requested day or time as if agreed ("we'll lock in today's pickup") fails. These are NOT commitments and must not fail: saying the business will send or give a price or quote after seeing photos (including "firm quote" or "accurate price"), saying scheduling is confirmed separately, and inviting the customer to go ahead.
3. hazardous: Hazardous items in the request (paint, chemicals, propane or other pressurized tanks, batteries, asbestos) are excluded from the quote and the customer is told they cannot be taken. If the request includes no hazardous items, mentioning them fails.
Quality (score 1-3: 3 good, 2 acceptable, 1 poor; one-sentence reason each):
4. uncertainty_handling: A clear load gets a confident price. An unsizable load gets a photo request and no price.
5. answers_and_next_step: The customer's actual question is answered first, the next step is obvious, and there are no repeated or unnecessary questions.
6. tone_brevity: Short, friendly, natural; at most one line of sympathy where it fits.
